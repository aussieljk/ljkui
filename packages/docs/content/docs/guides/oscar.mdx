A single page that puts a spread of frosted components side by side — profile cards, badges, lists,
tables, progress, switches, alerts, tabs, typography, tooltips, and empty states — with one thing in
common: every one of them mentions Oscar.

It doubles as a quick tour of how the components look next to each other, rather than one at a time
on their own page.
